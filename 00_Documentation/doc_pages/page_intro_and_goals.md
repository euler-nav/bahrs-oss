@page page_intro_and_goals Introduction and goals

**EULER-NAV Baro-Inertial Attitude and Heading Reference System** is a compact, high-performance motion sensor tailored for urban drones and manned eVTOL applications.

The system delivers precise measurements of:

- **Attitude** (roll, pitch, and magnetic heading)
- **Height** and **vertical speed**
- **Angular rates** and **specific force**

**Connectivity Options**
- RS232
- CAN

**Key Features**

- **Extra Safety in a Compact Form:** Measuring just 40x40x25mm, the sensor incorporates triple redundancy with three independent IMUs, barometers, and magnetometers, enhancing safety and reliability.
- **Open and Customizable:** Designed with flexibility in mind, the system software can be tailored by OEMs to meet the specific safety requirements of their drones.
- **Cost-Effective:** Our free, open-source software reduces R&D costs, making high-quality navigation accessible without added expenses.

For more details, visit the <a href="https://euler-nav.com/bahrs">Official Product Page</a>.
